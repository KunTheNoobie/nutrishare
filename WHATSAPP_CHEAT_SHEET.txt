🎯 *DESIGN PATTERN (OBSERVER):*
🗣️ *Look tutor in eye & say:* "The Observer Pattern is a design pattern where one main subject automatically notifies multiple listening observers whenever its data or state changes, without needing to know who they are."

🛡️ *SECURITY DEFENSES:*
• *Threat 1: CSRF (OWASP A05)*: Attacker creates hidden external form. Standard validation passes because form text is valid. Defense: `@csrf` token verified by Laravel `VerifyCsrfToken` middleware (HTTP 419 on failure).
• *Threat 2: Stored XSS (OWASP A07)*: Attacker inputs `<script>` into food description. Validation cannot ban `< >` because legitimate donors use them. Defense: Blade `{{ }}` automatically runs `htmlspecialchars()`.
